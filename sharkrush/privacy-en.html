Privacy Policy

Shark Rush: Match 3 · last updated October 4, 2026 · Русская версия

Who we are

Shark Rush: Match 3 is made by Styx Sloth Studios (Artem Andreevich Fedotov, self-employed). This page explains what data the game collects, why, who receives it, and what you can do about it. Questions: StyxSlothStudios@yandex.ru.

What data we collect

The game has no accounts and no paid purchases. Data falls into four groups.

On your device. Your progress and settings are stored on your phone. If Android's system backup is turned on, Android itself may save this file to the player's Google account — that's done by the operating system, not by the game.

Cloud copy of your progress. So that your progress survives uninstalling the game or switching phones, the game keeps a copy of it in Google Firebase's cloud database (Cloud Firestore). The copy is stored under a transfer code — 12 random characters that only you can see (Settings → "Saves"). The copy holds your game progress, coins, achievements, quests, collectibles and game settings. It contains no name, email, device identifiers or advertising identifier; like any server, Google sees your phone's IP address while the game talks to the database. Only someone who knows the code can read the copy.

Leaderboards. Your best results in the Daily Swim and the Endless Swim go to shared leaderboards in the same Google Firebase database, and every player can see a leaderboard row. A row holds a name the game made up from its own word lists (for example, "Grumpy Ray 4821") and the result: moves left or depth, the number of attempts that day, and when the record was set. You can't write any text of your own into a leaderboard. So that only you can change your row, the game creates an anonymous Firebase Authentication account — a random identifier with no name or email. It is stored in your save and moves to another phone with it via the transfer code.

Reminders. If you allow it, the game reminds you once a day, in the evening, about a new Daily Swim and your daily reward. The phone itself shows them on a schedule the game sets up: no data is sent anywhere for them. To turn them off — Settings → "Reminders" or your phone's notification settings.

Rating in RuStore. If you installed the game from RuStore, after a level win the game may ask you to rate it — the window is shown by RuStore itself. You leave your rating and review in RuStore under its rules; the game doesn't learn what you wrote. The RuStore reviews SDK built into the game, which opens that window, sends RuStore technical information about its own work: the SDK name and version and the app identifier. If the game was installed from another store, there is no such window, and the SDK sends nothing.

Through third-party SDKs — based on your choice.

  • Yandex Mobile Ads (rewarded and interstitial ads): advertising identifier, technical device data, IP address.
  • AppMetrica (analytics, also a Yandex service): device identifier and advertising identifier, game events (level start, level win and loss, ads, in-game coins spent, tutorial), crash reports.

Why

Ads are shown so you can get a reward for watching one, and so we can count how many ads were shown. To show and measure ads, Yandex Mobile Ads always uses the advertising identifier, technical data, and IP — without personalization. It will only tailor ads to your interests if you agreed to that.

Analytics shows us where players get stuck and which levels are too hard or too easy, so we can improve levels and game balance. AppMetrica receives nothing from the game at all until you give consent.

The cloud copy exists only to give your progress back: after reinstalling the game, or on a new phone with your transfer code. We don't use it for anything else.

Leaderboards let you compare your results with other players.

Who receives the data

Data that leaves your device only goes to the services we use:

  • Yandex Mobile Ads — Yandex's privacy policy: yandex.ru/legal/confidential; how it works in apps: ads.yandex.com/helpcenter.
  • AppMetrica — data processing details: appmetrica.yandex.com/docs.
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Authentication) — the cloud copy of your progress and the leaderboards; how Firebase handles data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • RuStore — the rating window for those who installed the game from RuStore; how the reviews SDK works: rustore.ru/help/sdk/reviews-ratings.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone besides these four services, and we don't get access to what's stored only on your device.

How long we keep data

Progress and settings on your device are kept as long as the game is installed, and disappear when you uninstall it. The cloud copy is kept until you delete it — surviving an uninstall is the whole point of it. Leaderboard rows and the anonymous account are kept until you delete them. Retention periods for Yandex Mobile Ads and AppMetrica are set by those services themselves — see the documents linked above.

Consent and how to withdraw it

On first launch, the game asks whether to allow personalized ads and analytics, or not. You can change your answer anytime — Settings → "Ads and analytics". Turning that switch off withdraws your consent: the game stops sending data to AppMetrica and stops showing personalized ads.

Your rights

If you play from Russia, you have rights under 152-FZ; if you play from the EEA, under the GDPR. Either way, you can:

  • find out what data we have about you;
  • ask us to correct it;
  • ask us to delete it;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • file a complaint with your country's data protection authority.

You can delete the cloud copy, your leaderboard rows and the anonymous account in the game itself: Settings → "Saves" → "Delete cloud data". Cloud save then turns off on that phone; you can turn it back on in the same place, with a new transfer code. If the same code is entered on another phone, delete the data there too — otherwise that phone will save its own copy to the cloud again.

To use your other rights, write to StyxSlothStudios@yandex.ru. Data on your device (progress, settings) is removed by simply uninstalling the game. If you no longer have the game but want the cloud copy and your leaderboard rows deleted, send us the transfer code: without it we can't find either.

Children

The game is not intended for children under 13.
